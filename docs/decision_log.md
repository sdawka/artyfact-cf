## 2026-10-12T18:31:11-04:00 — Parts and Things

A Thing can be an entire application and can start with only its Genome, without Parts or code. A System remains the purpose/governance wrapper; a one-Thing System opens that Thing directly while retaining its overview. Parts are reusable, partial or complete definitions at any scale, with shared library and System-private scope. Composition saves an ordinary Thing plus provenance; explicit selection, masking, replacement, commands, and upgrades resolve overlap without live inheritance. Source and realized artifacts are distinct aspects of the Thing, with runtime evidence attached through verified lineage. CLI/agents author; browser views inspect.
